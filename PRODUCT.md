# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Content creators, organic food vloggers, wellness advocates, chefs, and podcasters editing content related to food, lifestyle, nature, and the environment. They value a clean, calming, and professional interface that reflects their organic brand identity and makes video clipping a breeze.

## Product Purpose
ShortAI is an intuitive clipping platform that extracts the most impactful moments from long videos and podcasts, generating engaging word-by-word subtitles. Success means creating clean, professional, fully-captioned short-form video content within a calming, clutter-free, and natural workspace in under 2 minutes.

## Brand Personality
Calm, organic, professional, and natural. Three-word personality: Fresh, Earthy, Precise. It feels like a high-end cooking or wellness studio—grounded, warm, and highly efficient.

## Anti-references
- Dark, flashing neon gamer screens.
- Over-designed, heavy glassmorphism cards.
- Industrial dark gray dashboard interfaces that feel cold or clinical.
- Saturated red or hot-pink warning alerts that evoke high stress.

## Design Principles
- **Organic Harmony**: Use soft beige backgrounds, forest greens, and warm creams to ground the workspace in nature, reducing eye strain and workspace anxiety.
- **Utilitarian Clarity**: Clear visual borders and readable typography stacks ensure creators can slice, trim, and caption content with high accuracy.
- **Fluid Simplicity**: Maintain a smooth workflow where video content stands out against warm cream containers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict WCAG AA compliance with dark green typography on warm beige backgrounds for high contrast and readability.
- Clear keyboard navigation paths across timeline tracks.
- Focus rings highlighted in rich leaf green (#306D29) for accessibility.
